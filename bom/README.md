historical FB1 lands; read the assembly note rather than assuming a standard

and time/current curve are unrecorded. This is not evidence of a continuous
25 A test. Choose protection for the actual circuit within all component limits.

qualified board-current variant and requires different current-sense calibration.

Other manufacturer and supplier fields are retained procurement references,
not proof of every historical fitted part. U1 tape/reel procurement is a current
reference, not a claim about the packaging originally purchased. Mechanical
mounting features H1–H4 are part of the PCB, excluded from the electronic BOM
and placement file.
